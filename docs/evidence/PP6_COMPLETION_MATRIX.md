## Scope conflict: inactive materialization versus activated recovery

The original #975 goal and #1086 acceptance require **fresh-host restore activation**: old controller identity/leases must be invalidated, the source must be independently fenced, writers excluded, the restored controller handed off under a boot-bound guard, and reconciliation must observe unknown outcomes before it can dispatch mutations. The A10.1 amendment proposes a narrower **inactive-materialization-only** branch. Its terminal `INACTIVE_VERIFIED` state proves materialization/inventory only and must never grant controller, API, reconciliation, provider, or external mutation authority. Therefore A10 Phase C cannot satisfy or close the original #1086/#975 activation acceptance. Full PP.6 remains blocked unless the separately authorized activation boundary is implemented and evidenced, or the owner explicitly amends the #975/#1086 scope. No scope amendment or owner decision is inferred here.
